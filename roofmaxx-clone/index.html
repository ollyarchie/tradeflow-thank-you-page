Why Choose Us

The Smart Alternative to Replacement

Day and night, natural oils in your asphalt allow shingles to expand and contract as they protect your home. After years of sun and weather, those oils evaporate. That's where Thomas Sharps comes in.

100% Plant-Based

Our all-natural, plant-based oil restores flexibility in aging shingles, making them act like new again. Safe for people, pets, plants, and the planet.

Save Up to 80%

Why pay tens of thousands for a replacement when you can restore your roof for a fraction of the cost? Add up to 15 years with treatments every 5 years.

Backed by Science

Ohio State University tests proved that shingles restored with Thomas Sharps pass the same flexibility tests required for brand-new shingles.

5-Year Guarantee

Every treatment comes with a fully transferable 5-year warranty. Sell your home? The warranty goes with it, adding value to your sale.

Eco-Friendly

Each application saves 3.8 tons of waste from landfills and reduces CO2 emissions by over 1,500 kilograms. Lower your carbon footprint while saving money.

Quick & Non-Disruptive

The entire process takes just a couple of hours. No construction, no dumpsters, no noise — just a beautiful, restored roof.
